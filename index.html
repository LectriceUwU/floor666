Floor 666 A Little Bit of Everything

The first-ever Despot's Game mod is here! A little bit of everything, a whole lot of chaos.

This mod introduces new content, mechanics and improvements, but its main attraction is Floor 666, an entirely new and exclusive floor that replaces the original Easy mode.

Note Since other game modes interact with King of the Hill (KoTH), the following features are exclusive to Floor 666 unless otherwise stated.
